# Franchise Ledger Methodology v0.1

## Purpose

Franchise Ledger grades sports organizations across history.

It does not only ask who won the most. It asks how well each organization functioned over time.

## Core Indexes

### Legacy Score

All-time organizational achievement.

Primary factors:
- Regular-season strength
- Postseason achievement
- Sustained contention
- Era dominance
- Cultural significance
- Long-term stability

### Current Score

Present-day organizational health.

Primary factors:
- Major-league roster strength
- Farm system quality
- Player development
- Front-office quality
- Contract/payroll position
- Ownership behavior

### Momentum Score

Direction of travel.

Primary factors:
- Three-year trend
- Five-year trend
- Prospect pipeline movement
- Roster age curve
- Recent decision quality
- Contract burden

## v0.1 Composite Formula

Franchise Score =
  30% regular-season strength
+ 25% postseason achievement
+ 15% sustained contention
+ 10% player development
+ 10% roster/payroll efficiency
+ 5% cultural weight
+ 5% stability
- dysfunction penalty

The objective model should carry most of the score.

Analyst adjustment is allowed but capped:

-5 to +5 points

Every analyst adjustment must include a written reason.
